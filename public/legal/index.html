What this site does with your data

This site has no accounts and no uploads. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and does no tracking. It loads no fonts and no scripts from other hosts; the font is served from this site.

When you import your own songs, a CSV file you choose is read in your browser; only up to 30 song lines from it, or from a list you paste, are sent to api.fiveguesses.app to be matched to song previews.

Data stored in your browser

The game keeps its state in your browser's local storage under these keys:

  • fg:stats:v1: your statistics and streak.
  • fg:round:v2:<day>: today's round, under the number of the day's puzzle.
  • fg:theme:v1: your theme choice.

The local storage data never leaves your device. To delete it, open About in the game and choose Reset my data, or clear this site's data in your browser's settings.

In a room, the session storage of the tab holds fg:room:<code>: a random seat token for that tab, so a reload keeps your place. It is sent only to api.fiveguesses.app to find your seat and is gone when the tab closes.

Server logs

This site runs on a server rented from Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany) and administered by the operator. The web server writes access logs that hold your IP address, the time, the requested URL, the response status and your browser's user agent. IP addresses are shortened after at most 7 days. Log files are deleted after at most 5 weeks. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: the secure operation of the site.

Requests to Apple

When you press play, the song preview is loaded from audio-ssl.itunes.apple.com. When a round ends, the album artwork is loaded from a host under mzstatic.com. The link "Listen on Apple Music" opens music.apple.com in a new tab. In free play and in rooms, the text you type into the guess box is sent to itunes.apple.com for song suggestions, and their covers load from mzstatic.com. Apple then sees your IP address; Apple's privacy policy is at apple.com/legal/privacy/. Apart from these and api.fiveguesses.app (see below), the site makes no requests to other hosts, and its Content-Security-Policy allows no others.

Rooms and playlist import

Rooms, song categories and playlist import use api.fiveguesses.app, which runs on Cloudflare Workers (Cloudflare, Inc.; privacy policy at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver these requests. The service writes no logs of its own and has no accounts.

A room holds the names entered, the game progress and the seat tokens only while it exists. It deletes them when the game is over (10 minutes after the results), after 15 minutes without activity, or 5 minutes after everyone left.

Categories, charts and imports are looked up by the server at Apple (iTunes Search API, Apple Music playlist pages and Apple's chart feed), so Apple sees the server, not you.

YouTube

YouTube playlist import uses YouTube API Services. Our server reads the titles of the playlist you link, matches them to Apple Music previews and keeps nothing. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Article 15), to rectification (Article 16), to erasure (Article 17), to restriction of processing (Article 18), to data portability (Article 20) and to object (Article 21). To use them, write to oss@luminare.tech. You can also complain to the Austrian data protection authority, the Datenschutzbehörde (dsb.gv.at).

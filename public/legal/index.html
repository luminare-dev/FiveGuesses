What this site does with your data

This site has no accounts and no uploads. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and does no tracking. It loads no fonts and no scripts from other hosts; the font is served from this site.

Data stored in your browser

The game keeps its state in your browser's local storage under these keys:

  • fg:stats:v1: your statistics and streak.
  • fg:round:v2:<day>: today's round, under the number of the day's puzzle.
  • fg:theme:v1: your theme choice.

This data never leaves your device. To delete it, open About in the game and choose Reset my data, or clear this site's data in your browser's settings.

Server logs

This site runs on a server rented from Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany) and administered by the operator. The web server writes access logs that hold your IP address, the time, the requested URL, the response status and your browser's user agent. IP addresses are shortened after at most 7 days. Log files are deleted after at most 5 weeks. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: the secure operation of the site.

Requests to Apple

When you press play, the song preview is loaded from audio-ssl.itunes.apple.com. When a round ends, the album artwork is loaded from a host under mzstatic.com. The link "Listen on Apple Music" opens music.apple.com in a new tab. Apple then sees your IP address; Apple's privacy policy is at apple.com/legal/privacy/. These are the only requests to other hosts, and the site's Content-Security-Policy allows no others.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Article 15), to rectification (Article 16), to erasure (Article 17), to restriction of processing (Article 18), to data portability (Article 20) and to object (Article 21). To use them, write to oss@luminare.tech. You can also complain to the Austrian data protection authority, the Datenschutzbehörde (dsb.gv.at).
